```
Hi {NAME},

3. On proceeding, I introduce you to our Lender who reviews your position and prepares your Letter of Intent
4. With the Letter of Intent in hand, you submit the deposit and firm up the purchase (takes 2 to 3 weeks)

The initial process costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. It just lets me reserve the allocation in your name while the Lender pre-qualifies You and the Project.
```

**Placeholders / swap rules:**
- `{NAME}` — recipient's first name (the only thing to change by default).
- Property ("the 8 Plex") and the form link — swap only if Kunal names a different property/form.

**Why this wording (do not "improve" without owner sign-off):**
- Letter of Intent is only *named* as a step (the Lender prepares it) — never portrayed as confirming/guaranteeing the financing structure.
- "pre-qualifies You and the Project" (not "qualifies") — reads as pre-approval, not a guarantee.
- "costs you nothing and commits you to nothing" — low-pressure, accurate.
- No em dashes (WhatsApp/conversational rule).
