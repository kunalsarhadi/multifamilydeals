The eight months

From firm deal to keys, one stage at a time.

Every project runs the same arc. No single site below has been photographed at all nine stages, so each stop here is illustrated from whichever building was at that point when I visited.

Swipe the timeline
  1. Month 0
    Purchase & demolition
    The existing house comes down and the lot is cleared once the deal is firm.
    Photographed on site
  2. Month 1
    Excavation & foundation
    Dig, footings, walls poured, then services run to the building.
    Photographed at 9-Plex Inglewood
  3. Month 2
    Framing
    Main and upper floors stood and braced on the foundation.
    Photographed at 8-Plex Prince Charles
  4. Month 3
    Roof & envelope
    Trusses set, sheathing on, windows in, building wrapped.
    Photographed at 8-Plex Allendale
  5. Month 4
    Exterior & rough-ins
    Siding and trim outside; mechanical, electrical and insulation inside.
    Photographed at 9-Plex Inglewood
  6. Month 5
    Drywall
    Board, tape and paint, then interior doors hung.
    Photographed at 8-Plex Glenwood
  7. Month 6
    Interior finishing
    Kitchens, bath tile, flooring and fixtures land suite by suite.
    Photographed at 8-Plex Sherwood Park
  8. Month 7
    Site works
    Walkways formed and poured, parking and landscaping complete.
    Photographed at 8-Plex Sherwood Park
  9. Month 8
    Pre-leasing & handover
    Units advertised and leased ahead of closing, then keys.
    Photographed at 7-Plex Crawford Plains

Months are counted from a firm deal and describe the typical sequence, not a schedule. Construction timelines move with weather, trades, inspections, supply and municipal approvals, and an individual project can run materially longer or shorter than the eight months shown. Nothing here is a commitment to a completion date.
