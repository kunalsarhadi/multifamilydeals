The eight months

From firm deal to keys, one stage at a time.

Every project runs the same arc. No single site below has been photographed at all nine stages, so each stop here is illustrated from whichever building was at that point when I visited.

Swipe the timeline

  1. Month 0Purchase & demolition

    The existing house comes down and the lot is cleared once the deal is firm.

    Site clearing

  2. Month 1Excavation & foundation

    Dig, footings, walls poured, then services run to the building.

    9-Plex Inglewood

  3. Month 2Framing

    Main and upper floors stood and braced on the foundation.

    8-Plex Prince Charles

  4. Month 3Roof & envelope

    Trusses set, sheathing on, windows in, building wrapped.

    8-Plex Allendale

  5. Month 4Exterior & rough-ins

    Siding and trim outside; mechanical, electrical and insulation inside.

    9-Plex Inglewood

  6. Month 5Drywall

    Board, tape and paint, then interior doors hung.

    8-Plex Glenwood

  7. Month 6Interior finishing

    Kitchens, bath tile, flooring and fixtures land suite by suite.

    8-Plex Sherwood Park

  8. Month 7Site works

    Walkways formed and poured, parking and landscaping complete.

    8-Plex Sherwood Park

  9. Month 8Pre-leasing & handover
    No photo yet

    Units advertised and leased ahead of closing, then keys.

    7-Plex Crawford Plains

Months are counted from a firm deal. Construction schedules move with weather, trades and inspection dates — this is the sequence, not a completion commitment.
